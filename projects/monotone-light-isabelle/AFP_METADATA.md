Title: Monotone-Light Factorization

Authors: Arthur Freitas Ramos; David Barros Hulak; Ruy Jose Guerra Barretto de Queiroz.

Abstract: Every continuous map from a compact Hausdorff space to a Hausdorff space factors through the quotient by connected components of its point fibres. The quotient is compact Hausdorff, its projection is continuous, surjective and monotone, and the induced map is continuous and light. The factorization is unique up to a commuting homeomorphism among factorizations with a Hausdorff middle space, a continuous surjective monotone first map and a continuous light second map.
