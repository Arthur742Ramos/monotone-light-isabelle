The claim

Statement

Every continuous f from compact Hausdorff X to Hausdorff Y factors through the component quotient as a surjective monotone quotient q and continuous light l.

The plan

Informal proof

f is constant on q-fibres, so the quotient lifting theorem supplies a continuous l with f = l composed with q. If C is a connected subset of an l-fibre, its preimage under the closed monotone q is connected. That preimage lies in one f-fibre, hence in one component, and therefore C contains at most one point. This proves lightness.
